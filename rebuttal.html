Experiment 1: Rubber Duck vs. Metal Can

PIXIE is run independently on each object, then both are instantiated in a shared MPM scene.
Duck: E = 4×104 Pa, jelly (hyperelastic)  |  Can: E = 1×108 Pa, metal (elastoplastic)
The can tips over rigidly; the duck deforms on contact — behaviors emerge from PIXIE's predictions, not hand-tuning.

Experiment 2: Eight Rubber Ducks

Eight duck instances, all with the same PIXIE-predicted parameters, launched toward each other.
All ducks: E = 4×104 Pa, jelly (hyperelastic)
Natural elastic collisions and deformation throughout — PIXIE's predictions compose cleanly across multiple objects.
